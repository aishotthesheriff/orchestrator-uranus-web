# Quest to Uranus - Player Guide

## Welcome, Captain!

You've been given command of an underqualified spaceship with a dysfunctional crew on a critical mission: deliver paperwork to Uranus. What could possibly go wrong?

Quest to Uranus is a comedic text adventure where you navigate cosmic bureaucracy, uppity appliances, and the laws of physics (when they feel like cooperating). Each game takes 10-12 minutes, with your choices shaping an AI-generated story.

## How to Play

### Starting Your Journey

1. **Open the game** - You'll be greeted by your ship and crew
2. **Answer pre-flight questions (optional)** - Personalizes the story
3. **Read the opening narrative** - Sets the scene for your voyage
4. **Choose an action** - Click one of the 6 action buttons
5. **Watch the chaos unfold** - The AI narrates what happens
6. **Manage your resources** - Keep an eye on your ship's stats
7. **Survive (or don't)** - Reach Uranus or die hilariously trying

## Understanding Your Ship

### Stats (The Bad News)

Your ship has **five critical stats** that range from 0-100. When any reaches **zero**, it's game over (with a comedic death scene).

#### 🚀 Hull (Ship Integrity)
- **What it is:** Structural integrity of your ship
- **Drains from:** Collisions, asteroid strikes, physics experiments gone wrong
- **Warning:** Below 40 = hull breaches likely
- **Game over:** Explosive decompression (very messy)

#### 💨 Oxygen (Life Support)
- **What it is:** Breathable air supply
- **Drains from:** Life support malfunctions, crew breathing (the nerve!)
- **Warning:** Below 40 = everyone gets loopy
- **Game over:** Suffocation (surprisingly undignified)

#### ⛽ Fuel (Propulsion)
- **Drains from:** Movement, maneuvers, thrusters you forgot to turn off
- **Warning:** Below 40 = limited mobility
- **Game over:** Stranded in space (eternal drift)

#### 🍔 Food (Sustenance)
- **What it is:** Crew provisions
- **Drains from:** Eating, cooking disasters, mystery spoilage
- **Warning:** Below 40 = crew gets hangry
- **Game over:** Mutiny over the last granola bar

#### ❤️ Morale (Crew Happiness)
- **What it is:** How much your crew tolerates this mission
- **Drains from:** Bad events, failed checks, existential dread
- **Warning:** Below 40 = team friction grows; below 30 often increases difficulty
- **Game over:** Crew quits, demands to be dropped at nearest space station

### 👁️ Weirdness Meter (The Wild Card)

Unlike other stats, **Weirdness** goes from 0-5 and measures cosmic strangeness.

- **0-1:** Normal space travel (boring)
- **2-3:** Mild oddities (talking appliances, bureaucratic snails)
- **4-5:** Reality is negotiable (quantum phenomena, time loops)

**Effects:**
- Higher weirdness = more absurd events
- Can trigger special encounters
- Some items reduce weirdness
- Extreme weirdness can be good OR bad

**How it increases:**
- Wild actions
- Failed Vibes checks
- Story events
- Cosmic phenomena

## Your Crew (The Chaos Agents)

You have **four crew members** who provide critical skill bonuses:

### 👩‍✈️ Pilot Lyra
- **Specialist Skills:** Piloting, Deception
- **Personality:** Competent navigator with uncanny ability to chart bureaucratic space-time
- **How she helps:** Bonuses to Piloting and Deception checks based on affinity

### 🔧 Engineer Grom
- **Specialist Skills:** Engineering, Bureaucracy
- **Personality:** Keeps ship running with duct tape, spite, and questionable degree
- **How he helps:** Bonuses to Engineering and Bureaucracy checks based on affinity

### 🩺 Doctor Moss
- **Specialist Skills:** Bureaucracy, Culinary
- **Personality:** Ship's doctor specializing in space medicine and existential dread
- **How they help:** Bonuses to Bureaucracy and Culinary checks based on affinity

### ☕ Coffee-9000
- **Specialist Skills:** Diplomacy, Vibes
- **Personality:** Sentient coffee maker with strong opinions on brewing and labor rights
- **How it helps:** Bonuses to Diplomacy and Vibes checks based on affinity

**Relationship System:**
Each crew member has an **affinity meter** (-3 to +3) and a status:

**Affinity Levels & Skill Bonuses:**
- **+2 to +3 (Crush):** Provides **+2 bonus** to their specialist skills
- **+1 (Friendly):** Provides **+1 bonus** to their specialist skills
- **0 (Indifferent):** No bonus or penalty
- **-1 to -3 (Rivalry):** Inflicts **-1 penalty** to their specialist skills

**How Affinity Changes:**
- When you succeed at a crew member's specialist skill: affinity +1
- When you fail at a crew member's specialist skill: affinity -1
- Affinity affects both the narrative AND your mechanical bonuses

**Example:** If Pilot Lyra has +2 affinity and you attempt a Piloting check, you get a +2 bonus to your roll!

### How Skill Checks Work

When you take an action, the game rolls a **d20** (20-sided die) behind the scenes:

1. **Base Difficulty (DC)** is set by action risk:
   - **Safe actions:** DC 10 (easy)
   - **Risky actions:** DC 14 (medium)
   - **Wild actions:** DC 18 (hard)

2. **Difficulty often increases** as the game progresses:
   - Every 3 turns, DC goes up by 1
   - If morale is low, checks can get harder (commonly below 30)

3. **You roll a d20** (invisible, deterministic)
   - Bonuses from equipped items are added
   - Bonuses from crew affinity are added
   - If you have **Advantage**, roll twice and take the higher

4. **Success if roll ≥ DC**, failure if roll < DC

**Example:**
```
You choose: "Repair the hull" (Safe, uses Engineering)
Base DC: 10
Current turn: 7 (so +2 to DC = 12)
Morale: 45 (no penalty)
Final DC: 12

You have Universal Allen Key equipped (+2 Engineering)
Engineer Grom has +2 affinity (Crush) = +2 Engineering bonus
Roll: 8
Final result: 8 + 2 (item) + 2 (crew) = 12
12 = 12 = SUCCESS ✅
```

**The narrative shows:**
> 🎲 Check: Engineering DC12 → Roll 10 ❌

**Result:** The hull repair fails, possibly making things worse!

### Understanding Advantage

Some items give **Advantage** on specific skill checks:
- You roll the d20 **twice**
- Take the **higher** of the two rolls
- Dramatically improves success chances

**Example:**
```
Quantum Whoopee Cushion equipped
Action uses Vibes skill
Roll 1: 8
Roll 2: 15
Final roll: 15 (much better!)
```

**Early Game (Turns 1-4):**
- Focus on avoiding failures
- Equip items matching upcoming action intents
- Keep Nebula Freshener for morale

**Mid Game (Turns 5-8):**
- Critical stats matter more
- Use Allen Key if Hull is low
- Save Cheesecake for Food emergencies

**Late Game (Turns 9-12):**
- DCs are highest
- Max out bonuses for risky actions
- Whoopee Cushion for desperate Vibes plays

**Don't forget to swap items!** You can change equipment between turns.

## Action Types & Risk

Each turn, you get **6 action options** in 3 risk categories:

### 🟢 Safe Actions (2 per turn)
- **DC 10** - Easy to succeed
- **Low rewards** - Small stat changes
- **Low risk** - Failure isn't catastrophic
- **Examples:** "Check instruments", "File paperwork", "Make coffee"

### 🟡 Risky Actions (2 per turn)
- **DC 14** - Medium difficulty
- **Better rewards** - Significant stat improvements
- **Moderate risk** - Failure has consequences
- **Examples:** "Repair hull breach", "Negotiate with alien", "Cook elaborate meal"

### 🔴 Wild Actions (2 per turn)
- **DC 18** - Very hard
- **Best rewards** - Major stat boosts or story progression
- **High risk** - Failure can be disastrous
- **Examples:** "Improvise quantum bypass", "Challenge captain to duel", "Taste the void"

**Risk vs. Reward:**
- Safe actions keep you alive but won't fix crises
- Risky actions are the bread and butter
- Wild actions can save or doom you - use wisely

### Custom Actions

Don't like the 6 options? **Type your own!**
1. Click "Custom Action" (or similar button)
2. Type what you want to do
3. The AI will categorize it and create a skill check
4. Narrative adjusts to your creativity

**Examples:**
- "Bribe the coffee machine with premium beans"
- "Tell the alien about Earth reality TV"
- "Manually reboot life support while holding breath"

## Procedural Events

Some turns trigger **special events** based on stats, segment, or chance. These show up in the narrative (e.g., "Asteroid Belt Surprise — Piloting challenge") and influence the story and checks. Recurrent events can become **running gags** the AI will call back to later.

## The Journey (Game Segments)

Your voyage has **four acts**, each with different vibes:

### 🚀 Departure (Days 1-3, Turns 1-3)
- **Setting:** Near Earth/Moon
- **Tone:** Bureaucratic comedy
- **Threats:** Paperwork, inspections, launch permits
- **NPCs:** Getting used to the mission
- **Strategy:** Build resources, establish crew relationships

### ☄️ Belt (Asteroid Belt) (Days 4-6, Turns 4-6)
- **Setting:** Between Mars and Jupiter
- **Tone:** Slapstick danger
- **Threats:** Asteroid dodging, mining accidents
- **NPCs:** Crew dynamics heat up
- **Strategy:** Preserve Hull, manage chaos

### 🌌 Outer Darkness (Days 7-9, Turns 7-9)
- **Setting:** Deep space beyond Jupiter
- **Tone:** Cosmic horror comedy (still PG-13)
- **Threats:** Isolation, weirdness spikes, time dilation
- **NPCs:** Strange behaviors emerge
- **Strategy:** Keep Morale up, embrace the weird

### 🪐 Approach to Uranus (Days 10-12, Turns 10-12)
- **Setting:** Uranus orbit
- **Tone:** Climactic absurdity
- **Threats:** Final boss energy (bureaucratic or cosmic)
- **NPCs:** Relationships matter here
- **Strategy:** Survive final turns, deliver the paperwork

## Settings & Customization

### Absurdity Dial (⚙️ Settings)

Controls how spicy the humor gets (0-3):

- **0 - Dry Wit:** Very British, minimal innuendo
- **1 - Mild:** PG-13 sitcom energy
- **2 - Medium:** Beavis & Butt-Head meets Hitchhiker's Guide (default)
- **3 - Maximum:** Push PG-13 boundaries (never crosses them)

**What it affects:**
- Narrative tone
- Frequency of juvenile humor
- Running gag intensity
- NPC personality quirks

**Can be changed mid-game!** Open settings and adjust.

### Pre-Flight Interview (Optional)

Before launch, you may answer a few short questions. Your answers are remembered and referenced in narration and choices. If you skip, the game proceeds normally.

### Optional Scene Images

Some builds can generate **AI scene images** that reflect the current narrative and stats. These are cosmetic and don't affect gameplay.

## Winning & Losing

### Victory Conditions

**Complete the mission:**
- Survive all 12 turns
- Reach Uranus
- Deliver the paperwork
- Don't die

**Reward:** Epic finale narration based on:
- Remaining stats
- Crew relationships
- Weirdness level
- Your journey's chaos

### Failure States (Game Overs)

Any stat hitting **zero** triggers a comedic death:

- **Hull = 0:** Explosive decompression
- **Oxygen = 0:** Suffocation (but make it funny)
- **Fuel = 0:** Eternal drift in space
- **Food = 0:** Mutiny over rations
- **Morale = 0:** Crew abandons ship

Each failure gets a custom narrative based on how you died.

### Tips for Success

1. **Watch critical stats** (below 40) and prioritize them
2. **Balance risk** - don't spam Safe actions, but don't go full Wild
3. **Use items tactically** - swap before important turns
4. **Build crew relationships** - they help in narratives
5. **Embrace failure** - Comedic deaths are half the fun
6. **Adapt to segments** - Each act has different priorities
7. **Read the narrative** - Clues about what's coming next
8. **Manage Weirdness** - High weirdness is fun but unpredictable
9. **Save Morale** - Low morale makes everything harder
10. **Try custom actions** - AI responds to creativity

## Troubleshooting

**"I keep dying on Turn 6"**
- Prioritize Hull/Oxygen in Belt segment
- Use Safe actions when stats are critical
- Equip defensive items (Allen Key, Red Tape)

**"My crew hates me"**
- Choose actions that align with NPC skills
- Succeed on checks involving their specialties
- Use Nebula Freshener during crew scenes

**"Weirdness is out of control"**
- Equip Red Tape Dispenser (reduces weirdness)
- Avoid Wild actions for a few turns
- Choose bureaucratic/technical solutions

**"Actions don't match the situation"**
- This can happen - AI is creative
- Use custom action to do exactly what you want
- Report weird mismatches (it helps tune prompts)

## Speedrun Tips

For those chasing completion times:

1. **Risky actions only** - Skip Safe when possible
2. **Optimize item swaps** - Change every turn for max bonuses
3. **Build Engineer affinity early** - Helps with Hull management
4. **Target 80+ stats** at turn 6 - Cushion for late game
5. **Embrace Wild actions turns 9-12** - Need big wins to finish strong

## FAQs

**Q: Are dice rolls random?**
A: They're deterministic (seeded). Same game seed = same rolls. But you don't know the seed, so it feels random.

**Q: Can I save my game?**
A: Games auto-save after each turn. Closing the browser and returning resumes your session.

**Q: What if I want to restart?**
A: Click the 🔄 "New Voyage" button in the top-right.

**Q: How long is one game?**
A: 10-12 minutes on average (8-12 turns).

**Q: Is there multiplayer?**
A: No, it's single-player. But you can share funny death screens!

**Q: Can I mod the game?**
A: Prompts are editable in Dev Tools (`/dev-tools`). See the Dev Tools Prompts tab.

**Q: What's the optimal strategy?**
A: There isn't one! Chaos is part of the fun. Try different approaches.

**Q: Will there be updates?**
A: Check the project repository for new features, items, and events.
